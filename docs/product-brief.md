# Product brief

## Current product — 2026-09-06

The homepage presents eight giant breeds as reversible watercolor cards, with short history, origin, size and lifespan. Physical deck navigation replaces the shader wash. Discovery and ALL breed detail pages remain, including their shared assets and preparation guide. Discontinued games, postcards, places/public APIs, Kakao maps and old-home-only features have been removed by user approval.

## Product

Dog Atlas is a living breed atlas for people considering life with a dog.
It helps visitors discover unfamiliar breeds, understand how history and original roles relate to behavioral tendencies and modern care needs, and narrow their search to realistic conditions and candidates.

## Primary user

Someone who wants a dog but has limited knowledge and may currently choose mainly by appearance, popularity, or familiarity.

## Desired change

1. “I did not know this dog existed.”
2. “Its history explains some likely tendencies.”
3. “Living together requires this time, environment, and care.”
4. “I should compare several candidates and understand my own constraints.”

## Product voice

Curiosity and cuteness invite the user in. Clear, sourced information supports a responsible decision. Delaying adoption is a valid outcome.
